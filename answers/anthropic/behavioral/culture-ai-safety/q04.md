My previous research is not directly related to AI safety, but I can share some experience related. When I was doing image generation research, we usually optimize for generation FID. But at some time we discover that, a lower FID started to not necessarily reflect the generation quality. For example, for a L-size and XL-size model, they might have the same FID while a XL model gives a better generation quality when we look at generated images. Then I realized that it's as important to setup benchmarks that can really reflect model ability as improving the model ability, otherwise, it would be difficult for us to realize a large improvement (say, for RSP). Later, we also try to move to more reliable benchmarks such as FD_DINO.
