I make analogy of AI safety as educating a child, and education is not doubt important. Most human children are educated under a controlled setting, at least in a human environment. They learn to live with people, and at the same time learning knowledge. Even then, some "unsafely educated" people can conduct bad things. But AI is totally different. AI training is first fed with massive knowledge, where there definitely contains bad data. They are far more powerful than us, and we cannot afford any possibility that it exhibit bad behaviors.
